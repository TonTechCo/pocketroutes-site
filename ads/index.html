Apps refresh at launch, on resume and every five minutes. A visible ad can finish. Percentages control provider selection for eligible ad opportunities; actual impressions also depend on fill. There is no silent fallback to the other provider.

NUVIAD hint ads use interstitial inventory. The hint is earned after five seconds of viewing in the foreground and dismissal. Early close or failed creative loading gives no hint. The installed app must include NUVIAD support before allocating traffic to it. NUVIAD RTB requests do not require an API key. Confirm ad fill and placement setup before switching traffic.
